How this is counted

  • One account per person per platform. We track each politician’s personal or political account. Official government accounts, such as a governor’s, senator’s or representative’s office account, are never counted. Where a second account is prominent it is named in the notes below.
  • Followers, not engagement. Facebook figures are the larger of the page’s likes (from Social Blade) and its followers (checked by hand). YouTube figures are channel subscribers. TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and often Facebook display rounded figures (“5.2M”); those are recorded as shown and marked ≈. Small weekly changes on rounded figures can be noise.
  • “No account” means we found no active personal account on that platform. It counts as zero in the combined total.
  • Combined audience double-counts people. Someone who follows a politician on two platforms is counted twice. It measures reach across platforms, not unique people.
  • Snapshots are weekly. Counts are collected once a week: X and TikTok from public profile data (exact counts), YouTube from the channel page (rounded by YouTube), and Facebook and Instagram from the Social Blade and Instastatistics trackers. The source for every figure is kept in the data file.
